# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction
Premium scroll-driven cinematic landing page for tdotssolutionz.com. 4 scroll "acts" with dramatic reveal transitions between them. Editorial luxury feel — charcoal, white, cobalt blue, warm orange. No Three.js on the hub — pure CSS/GSAP for instant load. Seedance-generated orbit videos as backgrounds. The scroll IS the experience.

## Reference Points
- Twitter viral design posts showing silhouette-transform-ease-out scroll reveals
- Sites that make you go "woah" when you scroll (30K budget feel)
- Real CN Tower LED lighting patterns (multicolor cascade)
