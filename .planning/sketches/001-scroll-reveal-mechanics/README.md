# Sketch 001: Scroll Reveal Mechanics

## Design Question
What's the signature scroll-to-reveal transition between the 4 acts of the landing page?

## How to View
open .planning/sketches/001-scroll-reveal-mechanics/index.html

## Variants
- **A: Silhouette Clip-Path** — CN Tower + Toronto skyline silhouette rises up on scroll, revealing next section beneath. Tower pod has LED glow that activates during the transition. Most Toronto-specific.
- **B: Shadow Dissolve** — Dark panels split apart like curtains (left/right) revealing content beneath. Film-like, dramatic, less city-specific but more versatile.
- **C: Layer Peel** — Sections physically rotate up on their top edge with 3D perspective, like peeling back pages. Most tactile/physical feeling.

## What to Look For
- Which reveal feels most "premium" and unexpected?
- Does the silhouette (A) feel too gimmicky or does it nail the Toronto identity?
- Is the shadow dissolve (B) too subtle / too similar to what other sites do?
- Does the layer peel (C) feel like a real 30K site or more like a CSS experiment?
- How do the card entrance animations feel in each variant?
- Which would look best with actual Seedance orbit videos behind it?
